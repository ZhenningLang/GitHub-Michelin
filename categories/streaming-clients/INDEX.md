## What belongs here

End-user **client apps** that play content from streaming platforms in place of the official app — alternative YouTube/BiliBili/SoundCloud front ends for phones, TVs or desktops, whether they scrape the site directly or go through a proxy front end. Tools whose primary job is saving files are in `media-download`; self-hosted media servers and codec tooling are out of scope here (see `media-processing`).
