## What belongs here

Skills that direct image models through prompts, style recipes or composition rules. The host supplies generation; image-generation applications and runtimes belong in [ai-design-generation](../../../ai-design-generation/INDEX.md). Executable HTML/canvas drawing skills stay in the [parent category](../INDEX.md).
