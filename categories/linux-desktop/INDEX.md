## What belongs here

Projects whose job is **giving you a whole working Linux desktop**: opinionated distributions, desktop installers and full dotfiles suites that choose and configure the window manager, shell, apps and theming together.

Not here:

- **A single terminal, launcher or desktop widget** — see `terminal-ui` or `desktop-launchers`.
- **Running macOS on PC hardware** — see `hackintosh`.
