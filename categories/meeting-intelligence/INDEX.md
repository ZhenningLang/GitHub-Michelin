## What belongs here

Applications whose subject is **the meeting itself**: capturing call audio/screen, real-time transcription surfaced to participants, in-call intelligence (coaching, agent/tool automation), and post-meeting summaries, action items, and workflow exports. Not raw ASR engines or speech toolkits (see [speech](../speech/INDEX.md) and [whisper](../media-processing/video-audio/speech-and-subtitles/whisper.md)), not document knowledge bases (see [knowledge-base](../knowledge-base/INDEX.md)), and not desktop GUI automation (see [desktop-automation](../desktop-automation/INDEX.md)).
